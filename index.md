## About Me

I am a master's student at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Yujiu Yang](https://sites.google.com/view/iigroup-thu/). I am also a research intern at **Shanghai AI Lab**.

My research focuses on the safety of LLM-based agents: how agents perceive risk, use tools, and act in long-horizon environments. I build benchmarks, evaluations, and guardrails for agent safety, proactive risk awareness, and alignment.

## Research Interests

- **Agent safety:** trajectory-level evaluation, diagnosis, and guardrails
- **Tool-use safety:** safe interaction between language-model agents and external tools
- **Proactive risk awareness:** recognizing risks before harm happens
- **Alignment:** scalable safety training and deployment for interactive agents
